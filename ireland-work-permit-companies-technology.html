Common questions

Which technology and software companies in Ireland hire workers from abroad?

367 of them received employment permits from January to October 2026. The ones with the most were Google Ireland Limited, QT Technologies Ireland Limited, Amazon Development Centre Ireland Limited.

Does a company on this list have a job for me?

Not necessarily. The list shows permits already issued. It does not show vacancies, so check each employer's careers page and the job ad.

Who applies for the employment permit?

Either the employer or the worker can apply to the Department of Enterprise, Tourism and Employment, and an application must be made at least 12 weeks before the job starts.
