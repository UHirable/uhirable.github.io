How many care homes and home care providers can sponsor a UK work visa?

2,048 organisations in this group are on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors dated 12 November 2026. 2,047 are licensed for the Skilled Worker route.

Where are most of them?

The towns and cities with the most are London, Birmingham and Leicester.

Does a place on this list mean there is a job for me?

No. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor workers. It does not show vacancies, and a licensed employer can advertise jobs that do not come with sponsorship.
