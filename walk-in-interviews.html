Walk-in interviews around the world

Hiring events you can turn up to this month, confirmed with the organiser.

Last updated: 11 November 2026. We list an event only when its date and place are published by the organiser, the employer or a named news outlet, and every card links to that source. Dates and venues can still change, so check the source page before you travel. A real employer never asks you to pay for an interview or a job.

What to bring to a walk-in interview

  • Five or more printed copies of your CV. Download a free CV template.
  • Your ID or passport, and copies of your certificates.
  • A short answer ready for "Tell me about yourself" and "Why this role?".
  • Arrive early. Many events stop taking candidates once they are full.

How to spot a fake walk-in

  • You are asked to pay for the interview, a uniform, training or a visa.
  • The only contact is a personal WhatsApp number and there is no company address.
  • The company's own website or social pages do not mention the event.
