Visa sponsorship companies in Preston

Employers in Preston licensed to sponsor a UK work visa.

391 organisations in Preston hold a Home Office licence to sponsor work visas, and 372 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route. This page lists them from the official register dated 12 November 2026.

390 of the 391 have an A rating, which is the full licence. An organisation can be licensed for more than one route, so the rows add up to more than the total.

A licence is not a job offer. Being on the register means the employer is allowed to sponsor. It does not mean it is hiring now, or that a particular vacancy comes with sponsorship. Always check the job ad or ask the employer.

How to use this list

  1. Find employers in your field. Start with the sector groups above, then look for vacancies on each employer's own careers page.
  2. Check the route. For most long-term jobs you need an employer licensed for Skilled Worker.
  3. Check the job too. The job must be eligible and meet the salary rules for the visa, not only the employer.
  4. Confirm on GOV.UK. The register changes often. Check the official file before you rely on a name here.

Common questions

How many companies in Preston can sponsor a UK work visa?

391 organisations with Preston as their town or city are on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors dated 12 November 2026. 372 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route.

Does a company on this list have jobs in Preston right now?

Not necessarily. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor workers. It does not show vacancies, and a licensed employer can advertise jobs that do not come with sponsorship.

Which kinds of employers sponsor visas in Preston?

Going by registered names, the largest group among A-rated Skilled Worker sponsors in Preston is nhs, hospitals and health, with 28 organisations. The list also covers many small and medium businesses in other sectors.

Why can I not find a company I know?

The register uses legal names, not brand names, and the town is the one the organisation registered with, often its head office. Search the full register by name to check.
