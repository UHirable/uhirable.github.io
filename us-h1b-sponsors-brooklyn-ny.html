  1. Target employers that sponsor often. A high number of certified applications means the employer files for foreign workers every year.

Common questions

Which companies sponsor H-1B visas in Brooklyn?

By certified applications from July 2025 to June 2026, the largest are Maimonides Medical Center, The Brookdale Hospital Medical Center and Boston Consulting Group, Inc.. 638 employers filed for jobs in Brooklyn in total.

What is an LCA and how is it linked to the H-1B visa?

A Labor Condition Application is filed by the employer with the Department of Labor before it can file an H-1B petition. It states the job, the place of work and the pay. A certified LCA does not guarantee a visa.

Does a company on this list have open jobs now?

Not necessarily. The data shows past filings, including extensions and transfers for current employees. Check the employer's careers page for current vacancies.
