Visa sponsorship companies in Reading

Employers in Reading licensed to sponsor a UK work visa.

903 organisations in Reading hold a Home Office licence to sponsor work visas, and 872 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route. This page lists them from the official register dated 11 November 2026.

902 of the 903 have an A rating, which is the full licence. An organisation can be licensed for more than one route, so the rows add up to more than the total.

A licence is not a job offer. Being on the register means the employer is allowed to sponsor. It does not mean it is hiring now, or that a particular vacancy comes with sponsorship. Always check the job ad or ask the employer.

Common questions

How many companies in Reading can sponsor a UK work visa?

903 organisations with Reading as their town or city are on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors dated 11 November 2026. 872 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route.

Does a company on this list have jobs in Reading right now?

Not necessarily. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor workers. It does not show vacancies, and a licensed employer can advertise jobs that do not come with sponsorship.

Which kinds of employers sponsor visas in Reading?

Going by registered names, the largest group among A-rated Skilled Worker sponsors in Reading is technology and software, with 88 organisations. The list also covers many small and medium businesses in other sectors.

Why can I not find a company I know?

The register uses legal names, not brand names, and the town is the one the organisation registered with, often its head office. Search the full register by name to check.
