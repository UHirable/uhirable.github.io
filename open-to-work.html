Questions and answers

What is Open to Work on U Hirable?

A free page where people who are open to work add a profile card, and companies browse the cards and contact them directly.

Is it free?

Yes. U Hirable does not charge candidates or employers.

How does a company contact a candidate?

Each card has a LinkedIn button and a professional summary, where the candidate says how to reach them. Any contact or agreement is between the company and the candidate.

What is shown on my profile card?

Your first name and one initial, job title, industry, country, years of experience, what you are open for, your skills, your summary and your LinkedIn link. There are no photos.

When does my profile appear?

After U Hirable reviews it.

Does U Hirable verify profiles or take part in hiring?

No. U Hirable is a free listing only. It does not verify profiles, take part in hiring, or act as an employer or agent.

How do I remove my profile?

Email hello@uhirable.com and ask for it to be removed. You can do this at any time.
