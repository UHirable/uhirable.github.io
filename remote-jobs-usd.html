Remote · Hourly contracts

Remote expert work for AI projects, paid by the hour

Mercor hires professionals to help train and evaluate AI systems in their own field. Listings are remote hourly contracts. This page explains how it works and what to check before you apply.

Apply on Mercor →

What is this work?

Mercor says it partners with AI labs and enterprises to train frontier models using human expertise. Experts work on projects that train and evaluate AI systems in their area of expertise: for example a finance expert flags where a model's output would not pass review.

Its public listings are marked Hourly contract and Remote, and each one shows an hourly rate. Mercor's listings also say the listed rate scale is for US-based applicants and that the rate offered depends on your professional background and location.

1. Check the location first

Not every listing is open to every country. Many listings show a "Location requirements" line, for example USA only, or Canada, UK and USA. Mercor says a listing that names a region is open only to candidates from that region, and a listing with no location is assumed open to applicants in the US, Canada, the UK or the EU.

Before you spend time on an application, open the listing and read its location line.

3. How to apply on Mercor

1

Upload your resume

The first step is your resume. Check it first with our free CV checker.

2

Confirm your location

You confirm where you work from. This is what Mercor checks against a listing's location requirements.

3

Take the short AI interview

Mercor's listings describe a short AI interview about your background. One listing says the resume, location and interview take about 20 minutes in total. Some listings use a short evaluation exercise or work trial instead.

4

Wait for a matching project

Some listings are standing "talent network" listings. Mercor says these stay open whether or not a matching project is running that week, and it draws from them when a project begins.

4. Before you apply

Good to know

  • Listings state that payments are weekly on Stripe or Wise, based on services rendered. Check which one works in your country before you start.
  • The work is contract work by the hour or by the task, not a permanent job.
  • Some listings give the expected hours per week. Read them before you apply.

Stay safe

  • Apply only on Mercor's own website. Never pay anyone to "get you in".
  • The application includes a recorded AI interview. Mercor confirmed a data breach in April 2026 and later said it had added security measures. Decide what you are comfortable sharing.
  • Upload documents only inside the official platform, never by email, WhatsApp or Telegram.
