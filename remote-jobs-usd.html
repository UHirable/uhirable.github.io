3. Before you apply

Tips that help you get matched

  • Write your CV in English and name your exact specialty (e.g. "IFRS reporting", "pediatric dentistry", "React and Node.js").
  • List degrees, licences and certifications clearly: they are the main filter.
  • Answer the assessment with real examples and numbers from your work.
  • Apply even if you're employed: hours are flexible and many people work evenings and weekends.

Stay safe

  • Only apply on Mercor's official website. Never pay anyone to "get you in".
  • Upload ID documents only inside the official platform, never by email, WhatsApp or Telegram.
  • Work availability changes by project. Keep your main job until the income is steady.
  • Check how you'll be paid and any local tax rules before you start.
