NHS trusts and hospitals that sponsor UK work visas

Licensed sponsors on the Home Office register, 11 November 2026.

311 NHS trusts, health boards and hospitals hold a Home Office licence to sponsor work visas, and 309 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route. This page lists them all, from the official register dated 11 November 2026.

We group organisations by words in their registered name, so this is a practical list and not an official classification. A few names may match by coincidence, and organisations that trade under a different brand name will be missing.

A licence is not a job offer. Being on the register means the employer is allowed to sponsor. It does not mean it is hiring now, or that a particular vacancy comes with sponsorship. Always check the job ad or ask the employer.

Common questions

How many NHS trusts, health boards and hospitals can sponsor a UK work visa?

311 organisations in this group are on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors dated 11 November 2026. 309 are licensed for the Skilled Worker route.

Where are most of them?

The towns and cities with the most are London, Liverpool and Manchester.

Does a place on this list mean there is a job for me?

No. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor workers. It does not show vacancies, and a licensed employer can advertise jobs that do not come with sponsorship.
