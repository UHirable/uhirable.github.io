Australia labour agreement employers

Check if an Australian employer holds a labour agreement to sponsor workers from overseas.

Australia publishes the businesses that hold a labour agreement, which lets them sponsor skilled workers from overseas. The official list has 6,382 current agreements held by 6,040 employers, dated 30 September 2026. Search them here by company name, or browse by agreement type.

    An agreement is not a job offer. A labour agreement lets the business sponsor skilled workers from overseas. It does not mean it is hiring now. This is the list of labour agreements only. It is not a list of every employer that can sponsor a visa in Australia.

    Designated Area Migration Agreements (DAMA)

    A DAMA is a formal agreement between the Australian Government and a state, territory or regional authority. It gives employers in that area access to more occupations than the standard skilled migration programme.

    What a labour agreement is

    Labour agreements let approved businesses sponsor skilled overseas workers when there is a workforce need that the Australian labour market cannot meet and the standard visa programmes are not available. Agreements are generally in effect for five years. Three visas can be granted under one.

    How to use the list in your job search

    1. Start with your industry or region. Open the agreement type that matches your work, then look for vacancies on each employer's own careers page.
    2. Check the end date. Each type page shows when the agreement ends.
    3. Confirm on the official site. Search the company on the Home Affairs list before you rely on it.
    4. Never pay for a job. A real employer does not charge you for a job offer or a visa.

    Common questions

    Which companies in Australia have a labour agreement to sponsor overseas workers?

    The Department of Home Affairs publishes them. Its list dated 30 September 2026 has 6,382 current agreements held by 6,040 employers, and you can search them all on this page.

    What is a labour agreement?

    An agreement that lets an approved business sponsor skilled overseas workers when the Australian labour market cannot meet the need and the standard visa programmes are not available.

    How long does a labour agreement last?

    Agreements are generally in effect for five years.

    Is this every employer that can sponsor a visa in Australia?

    No. It is the list of labour agreements only.

    Does being on the list mean the company is hiring?

    No. The list shows who holds an agreement. It does not show vacancies, so always check the employer's current jobs.
