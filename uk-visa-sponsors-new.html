Common questions

Which companies became UK visa sponsors in November 2026?

708 organisations joined the Home Office register of licensed sponsors between 12 November 2026 and 26 November 2026. They are all listed on this page.

Does a new sponsor have jobs with sponsorship right now?

Not necessarily. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor workers. It does not show vacancies, so check each employer's careers page and the job advert.

How often is this page updated?

At the start of every month, from the official GOV.UK file. The Home Office itself updates the register every working day.
