German CV format (Lebenslauf): what employers expect

A German CV follows its own rules. Here is the layout the Federal Employment Agency describes, in plain English.

Last checked: November 2026. This is general guidance. Employers say in each advert which documents they want, so follow the advert first. Official advice: Federal Employment Agency: the curriculum vitae

A German CV (Lebenslauf) is written in table form, is no longer than two pages, and lists your experience with the most recent first. A photo is not mandatory, but most employers still expect one. You finish the CV with the date and your signature, and you send it with a cover letter and copies of your certificates.

What goes in, in order

SectionWhat to write
Personal detailsName and contact details. A professional photo at the top of the CV or on a cover sheet is optional but widely expected. Marital status and religion are not required.
Professional experienceStart with your most recent job. Give the start and end dates (month and year is enough), the employer, your position, and three to five bullet points on your tasks.
Education and trainingVocational training or studies, then further training, most recent first.
SkillsLanguages, IT skills and other knowledge relevant to the job.
InterestsOnly if they relate to the job. Volunteer work and club memberships are well regarded.
Date and signatureClose the CV with the date and your signature.

Rules that are different from a UK or US CV

  • Table layout. Dates on the left, details on the right, in clear sections.
  • The photo. Employers are not allowed to demand one, but it is still common. Some employers ask for anonymised CVs with no photo.
  • Certificates. Attach copies of degrees, training certificates and reference letters from previous jobs.
  • Cover letter. About one A4 page, written for that job, explaining why you want the position and why you fit.
  • Language. Have the CV proofread by a native German speaker if you write it in German.
  • Europass. The Europass template is a solid base, but many employers prefer a more individual CV.

Questions people ask

Do I need a photo on a German CV?

It is not mandatory and employers may not demand it, but Germany's Federal Employment Agency says most employers still expect one. Use a professional photo at the top of the CV or on a cover sheet.

How long should a German CV be?

No longer than two pages, in table form, with the most recent entries first.

Do I have to sign a German CV?

Yes, it is the convention. End the CV with the date and your signature.

Can I apply in English in Germany?

Follow the language of the job advert. If the advert is in German, apply in German and have a native speaker proofread it.
