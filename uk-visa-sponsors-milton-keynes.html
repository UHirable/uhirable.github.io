Visa sponsorship companies in Milton Keynes

Employers in Milton Keynes licensed to sponsor a UK work visa.

836 organisations in Milton Keynes hold a Home Office licence to sponsor work visas, and 804 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route. This page lists them from the official register dated 12 November 2026.

835 of the 836 have an A rating, which is the full licence. An organisation can be licensed for more than one route, so the rows add up to more than the total.

A licence is not a job offer. Being on the register means the employer is allowed to sponsor. It does not mean it is hiring now, or that a particular vacancy comes with sponsorship. Always check the job ad or ask the employer.

Common questions

How many companies in Milton Keynes can sponsor a UK work visa?

836 organisations with Milton Keynes as their town or city are on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors dated 12 November 2026. 804 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route.

Does a company on this list have jobs in Milton Keynes right now?

Not necessarily. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor workers. It does not show vacancies, and a licensed employer can advertise jobs that do not come with sponsorship.

Which kinds of employers sponsor visas in Milton Keynes?

Going by registered names, the largest group among A-rated Skilled Worker sponsors in Milton Keynes is technology and software, with 57 organisations. The list also covers many small and medium businesses in other sectors.

Why can I not find a company I know?

The register uses legal names, not brand names, and the town is the one the organisation registered with, often its head office. Search the full register by name to check.
