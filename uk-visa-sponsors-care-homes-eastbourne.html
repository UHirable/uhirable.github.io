Care homes with visa sponsorship in Eastbourne

Care providers in Eastbourne on the Home Office register, 12 November 2026.

15 care homes, home care agencies and other care providers in Eastbourne hold a Home Office licence to sponsor work visas, and 15 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route, which includes the Health and Care Worker visa. This page lists them from the official register dated 12 November 2026.

Search all of the UKAll UK care homes

Important if you are outside the UK. Since 22 July 2025 the UK no longer accepts new applications from overseas for care worker and senior care worker jobs. People already in the UK can still switch into these jobs until 22 July 2028 if they have already worked for that employer for at least three months, and the government keeps this under review. Nurses and other eligible health roles can still be sponsored from abroad: see our UK nurse visa sponsorship guide.

We pick care providers by words in their registered name, such as care, caring, nursing home and homecare. This is a practical list and not an official classification: a few names may match by coincidence, and providers that trade under a different name will be missing. The town is the one the organisation registered with, often its head office.

A licence is not a job offer. Being on the register means the employer is allowed to sponsor. It does not mean it is hiring now, or that a particular vacancy comes with sponsorship. Always check the job ad or ask the employer.

How to use this list

  1. Check where you are applying from. New care worker and senior care worker applications from outside the UK closed on 22 July 2025.
  2. Go to the provider directly. Look for vacancies on its own site and ask whether the role comes with sponsorship.
  3. Never pay for a job or a certificate of sponsorship. The employer pays the sponsorship costs. A request for money is a warning sign.
  4. Confirm on GOV.UK. The register changes often. Check the official file before you rely on a name here.

Common questions

Which care homes in Eastbourne offer visa sponsorship?

15 care providers with Eastbourne as their town or city are on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors dated 12 November 2026. 15 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route. The full list is on this page.

Can I get a care worker job in Eastbourne with sponsorship from outside the UK?

Not as a new care worker or senior care worker. Since 22 July 2025 the UK does not accept new applications from overseas for those two jobs. Nurses and other eligible health roles can still be sponsored from abroad.

I am already in the UK. Can a care home sponsor me?

Until 22 July 2028 people already in the UK can switch into care worker roles if they have already worked for that employer for at least three months. The government keeps this under review, so check GOV.UK.

Does a place on this list mean there is a job for me?

No. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor workers. It does not show vacancies, and a licensed employer can advertise jobs that do not come with sponsorship.
