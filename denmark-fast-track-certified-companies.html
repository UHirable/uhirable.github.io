Denmark Fast-track certified companies

Check if a Danish employer can hire you from abroad through the Fast-track Scheme.

Denmark publishes the companies it has certified to hire workers from outside the EU through the Fast-track Scheme. There are 991 of them on the official list, updated on 21 October 2026. Search them here by name, or scroll the full list below. The scheme makes it faster for certified companies to recruit foreign employees.

United KingdomCanadaUnited StatesIrelandNetherlandsDenmarkAustraliaFinland

    Certification is not a job offer. Being on the list means the company may hire from abroad through the Fast-track Scheme. It does not mean it is hiring now. Always check the job ad or ask the employer.

    What the Fast-track Scheme is

    The Fast-track Scheme lets certified companies recruit foreign employees faster. Your employer submits the application for you, so you give them a power of attorney. Your job must fit one of five tracks.

    TrackMain condition in 2026
    Pay limitSalary of at least DKK 552,000 a year
    Supplementary pay limitSalary of at least DKK 446,000 a year, plus extra conditions such as advertising the job first
    Short-termA stay of up to 90 days
    ResearcherA research position
    EducationalA highly qualified training stay at the certified company

    SIRI adjusts the salary amounts every 1 January. The application fee is DKK 6,810, and your spouse or partner and children under 18 can get a permit to come with you. Check the current figures on nyidanmark.dk before you negotiate an offer.

    How to use the list in your job search

    1. Look for vacancies on the company's own careers page. Tap any name in the list to search for it.
    2. Check the salary. The offer must meet the amount for your track.
    3. Never pay for a job. A real employer does not charge you for a job offer or a permit.

    Common questions

    Which companies in Denmark can hire foreign workers through the Fast-track Scheme?

    Companies certified by SIRI, the Danish Agency for International Recruitment and Integration. There are 991 on the official list updated on 21 October 2026, and this page lists them all.

    What salary do I need for the Fast-track Scheme in 2026?

    At least DKK 552,000 a year on the pay limit track, or DKK 446,000 a year on the supplementary pay limit track, which has extra conditions.

    Who applies for the permit?

    Your employer. Under the Fast-track Scheme the certified company submits the application, using a power of attorney from you.

    Does being on the list mean the company is hiring?

    No. The list shows who is certified. It does not show vacancies, so always check the employer's current jobs.

    Sources

    • New to Denmark (SIRI): Certified companies
    • New to Denmark (SIRI): the Fast-track Scheme
    • New to Denmark (SIRI): certification of companies

    U Hirable is not connected to SIRI. The list changes when SIRI certifies or removes a company, so always confirm a company on nyidanmark.dk before you rely on it.
