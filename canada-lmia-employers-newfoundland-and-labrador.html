Employers in Newfoundland and Labrador approved to hire foreign workers.

72 employers in Newfoundland and Labrador were approved to hire foreign workers with a positive LMIA from July 2025 to June 2026, for 1,383 positions in total. This page lists them from the official Government of Canada list published on 30 October 2026.

How to use this list

  1. Pick employers that hired for your job. The last column shows the occupations each employer was approved for.
  2. Go to the employer directly. Look for current vacancies on the employer's own site or on a job site, and apply there.
  3. Never pay for an LMIA. The employer applies for it and pays the fee. A recruiter who asks you to pay for one is a warning sign.

Common questions

How many employers in Newfoundland and Labrador have a positive LMIA?

72 employers in Newfoundland and Labrador appear on the official positive LMIA list for July 2025 to June 2026, covering 1,383 approved positions.

Which jobs get the most LMIA approvals in Newfoundland and Labrador?

Counting employers, the most common occupations are fish and seafood plant workers, labourers in fish and seafood processing, harvesting labourers.

Does this list show jobs that are open now?

No. It is a record of past approvals in the last four published quarters. Always check the employer's current vacancies.

Who pays for the LMIA?

The employer applies and pays the processing fee. You should not be asked to pay for an LMIA.
