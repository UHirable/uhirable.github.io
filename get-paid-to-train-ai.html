Get paid to train AI: who qualifies, pay and countries

Mercor and Terac pay professionals to help train and evaluate AI systems. This page compares the two and shows what to check before you apply.

The short answer

You do not need to be an AI engineer. Both platforms look for people with real experience in a field, such as finance, law, medicine, engineering, marketing or product management. The work is reviewing, testing and improving what AI systems produce in your own field.

Check these three things first

1. The location line. Not every opening is open to every country. On Mercor, a listing that names a region is open only to candidates from that region. On Terac, a study that is not open in your country is marked that way.

2. The requirements. Terac studies ask for specific experience, for example two or more years of hands-on work in the field. Some ask for 20 or more hours a week.

3. Other AI training work you already do. Some Terac studies are not open to people already paid for AI training work by companies such as Scale AI, Outlier or Mercor. Read each study's requirements before you apply to both platforms.

Common questions

Do I need to be an AI engineer to get paid to train AI? No. Both platforms look for people with real experience in a field, such as finance, law, medicine, engineering, marketing or product management. The work is reviewing, testing and improving what AI systems produce in your own field.

Which countries can apply? It depends on the opening. On Mercor, each listing has a location line and some name a region, for example USA only. On Terac, each study names where you must be based; on 23 November 2026 many named the US, or the US, UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

How do Mercor and Terac pay? Mercor's listings state that payments are weekly, through Stripe or Wise. On Terac you withdraw your balance to your bank account: the minimum is $12.50, your identity is verified before the first withdrawal, and the money arrives in 1 to 3 business days.

Is it free to join? Yes. Signing up is free on both platforms. Never pay anyone to get this kind of work.

Is it a full-time job? No. Mercor's listings are hourly contracts and Terac's are studies, so the hours depend on the project.
