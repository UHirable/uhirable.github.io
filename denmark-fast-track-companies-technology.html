Common questions

Which technology and software companies in Denmark can hire foreign workers through the Fast-track Scheme?

80 of them are on the official SIRI list of certified companies, updated on 21 October 2026. This page lists them all.

Does a company on this list have a job for me?

Not necessarily. The list shows who is certified. It does not show vacancies, so check each employer's careers page.

What salary do I need for the Fast-track Scheme in 2026?

At least DKK 552,000 a year on the pay limit track, or DKK 446,000 a year on the supplementary pay limit track, which has extra conditions.
