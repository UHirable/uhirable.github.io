Logistics and Transport Companies in the Netherlands that sponsor visas

IND recognised sponsors for work, 3 November 2026.

221 logistics and transport companies are recognised sponsors of the IND for work and highly skilled migrants. That means they can hire people from outside the EU through the faster IND procedure. This page lists them all, from the official register updated on 3 November 2026.

United KingdomCanadaUnited StatesIrelandNetherlands

We group organisations by words in their registered name, so this is a practical list and not an official classification. A few names may match by coincidence, and companies that trade under a different name will be missing. Search the full register to check any company.

Recognition is not a job offer. Being on the register means the organisation is allowed to apply for residence permits for highly skilled migrants and other workers. It does not mean it is hiring now. Always check the job ad or ask the employer.

Common questions

Which logistics and transport companies in the Netherlands sponsor highly skilled migrants?

221 of them are on the IND public register of recognised sponsors for work, updated on 3 November 2026. This page lists them all.

Does a company on this list have a job for me?

Not necessarily. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor. It does not show vacancies, so check each employer's careers page.

Why does the employer need to be a recognised sponsor?

For the highly skilled migrant permit, only an IND-recognised sponsor can apply for you. If the company is not on the register, it cannot hire you on that permit.
