Remote jobs you can do from Egypt that pay in dollars

How people in Egypt get hired by companies abroad, how the contract and payment usually work, and where to find the jobs.

Last checked: November 2026. This page is a general guide, not tax, legal or financial advice. Tax and banking rules in Egypt change, so check your own case with an accountant or your bank before you sign a contract. Background reading: Deel: international taxes when working remotely

Yes. You can live in Egypt, work for a company in another country and be paid in US dollars. Companies usually hire you in one of two ways: as an independent contractor who sends invoices, or as an employee through an employer of record (EOR). Common remote roles include software development, customer support, marketing, sales, design and operations.

The two ways you get hired

Independent contractorEmployee through an EOR
What it isYou are self-employed. You sign a contractor agreement and invoice the company.A third-party company (the employer of record) hires you on the company's behalf under an employment contract.
TaxYou manage and pay your own taxes.The EOR runs payroll and handles tax and compliance.
BenefitsUsually none. You arrange your own.Statutory benefits and employment protections.
FlexibilityYou decide how and when you work and can have several clients.A defined role with one employer.

Neither is better for everyone. A contractor rate is often higher on paper, but you cover your own tax, insurance and time off. Ask which model the company uses before you compare offers.

What to ask before you accept

  • Currency. Is the pay written in US dollars in the contract, and is it paid in dollars?
  • Payment method. An international bank transfer to a dollar account, or a payment service that lets you hold dollars and withdraw to your local bank. Check the fees and that your bank is supported before you agree.
  • Contract type. Contractor or employee through an EOR, and who the contract is with.
  • Working hours. Egypt is on UTC+2, and UTC+3 during summer time. Ask how many hours must overlap with the team.
  • Payment schedule. Monthly or per invoice, and how many days after the invoice.
  • Equipment and notice period. Who provides the laptop, and how either side can end the contract.

Tax

As a general rule you pay income tax in the country where you live and work. If you are a contractor, the company does not withhold tax for you, so you pay it yourself. Rules and thresholds change, so confirm your own situation with an accountant in Egypt.

Protect yourself

  • Do not pay to get a job, an interview or "training".
  • Get a written contract before you start work, with the rate, currency and payment date.
  • Search for the company and the person who contacted you before you share documents.

Get your CV ready

Remote employers read your CV before anything else, often through an applicant tracking system. Write it in English, keep it to one or two pages, and match the words in the job ad.

Questions people ask

Can I work from Egypt for a foreign company and be paid in dollars?

Yes. Companies abroad hire people in Egypt either as independent contractors who invoice in US dollars or as employees through an employer of record. Confirm the currency and the payment method in the contract before you start.

What is an employer of record (EOR)?

A third-party company that legally employs you on behalf of the company you work for. It handles your employment contract, payroll, tax and benefits, while the other company manages your daily work.

Do I pay tax in Egypt on remote income?

As a general rule you pay income tax where you live and work, and a contractor pays it without the company withholding it. The details depend on your case, so check with an accountant in Egypt.

Which remote jobs can I do from Egypt?

Common remote roles include software development, customer support, marketing, sales, design and operations. Most adverts and interviews are in English.
