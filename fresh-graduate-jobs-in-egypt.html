Fresh graduate jobs in Egypt

What to search for, how to write a first CV, and current entry-level openings.

Last checked: November 2026. This page is practical job-search advice from a recruiter, not official statistics. The job cards below are live and change every day. CV advice follows the National Careers Service CV guidance.

Entry-level jobs in Egypt are rarely labelled "fresh graduate". Search for the words employers actually use: junior, trainee, intern, entry level, graduate programme and the job title you want. Put your education first on your CV, count internships, projects and volunteering as experience, and never pay for a job or an interview.

Search words that find entry-level jobs

junior trainee intern entry level customer service sales

Add a city to narrow it down, for example "junior accountant cairo".

A CV when you have no experience

  • Education first. If you are early in your career, put your education before your work history: degree, university and dates.
  • Count everything. Internships, work placements, volunteering, student activities and graduation projects all go under experience.
  • Two or three lines each. Say what you did and what came out of it.
  • A short introduction. A few lines under your name on who you are and the role you want.
  • One page. Clear font, same style throughout, no spelling mistakes.

Protect yourself

  • Do not pay for a job, an interview, a "training course" or a uniform before you start.
  • Search for the company before the interview and check that it has a real address.
  • Do not send your national ID or bank details before you have a written offer.

Questions people ask

What should I search for to find fresh graduate jobs in Egypt?

Search for junior, trainee, intern, entry level and graduate programme together with the job title you want. Many entry-level adverts do not use the words fresh graduate.

How do I write a CV with no work experience?

Put your education first, then list internships, placements, volunteering, student activities and projects as experience, with two or three lines on what you did in each.

Should I pay a company to get a job?

No. Do not pay for a job, an interview or training before you start.
