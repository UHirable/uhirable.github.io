LMIA employers hiring farm workers in British Columbia

Employers in British Columbia approved to hire foreign farm workers.

769 employers in British Columbia were approved to hire foreign farm workers with a positive LMIA from July 2025 to June 2026. Most are in Abbotsford, Chilliwack and Kelowna. This page lists them from the official Government of Canada list published on 30 October 2026.

How to use this list

  1. Pick employers that hired for your job. The last column shows the occupations each employer was approved for.
  2. Go to the employer directly. Look for current vacancies on the employer's own site or on a job site, and apply there.
  3. Never pay for an LMIA. The employer applies for it and pays the fee. A recruiter who asks you to pay for one is a warning sign.

Common questions

Which employers in British Columbia hire foreign farm workers with an LMIA?

769 employers in British Columbia on the official positive LMIA list for July 2025 to June 2026 had farm workers among their main approved occupations. The table on this page shows those with the most approved positions.

Which cities in British Columbia have the most LMIA employers for farm workers?

Counting employers, the top cities are Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Kelowna.

Does this list show jobs that are open now?

No. It is a record of past approvals in the last four published quarters. Always check the employer's current vacancies.

Who pays for the LMIA?

The employer applies and pays the processing fee. You should not be asked to pay for an LMIA.
