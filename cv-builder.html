How the free CV builder works

Type your details once and the preview updates as you go. Pick one of 5 designs (Classic, Slate, Crimson, Executive or Emerald), then download a Word file (editable) or a PDF (ready to send). Everything happens inside your browser: your CV is never uploaded to U Hirable or anyone else, nothing is kept after you close or refresh the page, and there is no account, watermark or paid tier.
