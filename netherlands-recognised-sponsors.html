Netherlands recognised sponsors (IND)

Check if a Dutch employer can sponsor your highly skilled migrant visa.

To hire you as a highly skilled migrant (kennismigrant), a Dutch employer must be a recognised sponsor of the IND. Search all 13,089 recognised sponsors for work here by company name. The list comes from the official IND register, updated on 3 November 2026.

    Recognition is not a job offer. Being on the register means the organisation is allowed to apply for residence permits for highly skilled migrants and other workers. It does not mean it is hiring now. Always check the job ad or ask the employer.

    How to use the register in your job search

    1. Check the employer before you apply. If a company offers you a highly skilled migrant job and it is not on the register, it cannot apply for your permit.
    2. Check the salary. The offer must meet the IND salary criterion for your age.
    3. Never pay for a job. A real employer does not charge you for a job offer or a permit.

    Common questions

    Which companies in the Netherlands can sponsor a work visa?

    Organisations recognised by the IND. There are 13,089 recognised sponsors for work on the public register updated on 3 November 2026, and you can search them all on this page.

    What is a recognised sponsor (erkend referent)?

    A company, school or organisation that the IND has recognised. For the highly skilled migrant permit, only a recognised sponsor can apply for you.

    Does being on the register mean the company is hiring?

    No. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor. It does not show vacancies, so always check the employer's current jobs.

    How often is the register updated?

    The IND updates its public registers once a month.
