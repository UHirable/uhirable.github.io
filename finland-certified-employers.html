What employer certification means

  • It proves that the company meets certain requirements as an employer.
  • It speeds up the processing of a residence permit application for an employed person.
  • You still apply for your residence permit yourself and must meet the requirements for the permit.
  • It does not change the fee for the residence permit application.
  • A first certification is granted for two years. After that it can be extended for three years at a time.

How to use the list in your job search

  1. Look for vacancies on the company's own careers page. Tap any name in the list to search for it.
  2. Use Finland's official job sites too. Migri points job seekers to Work in Finland and Job Market Finland.
  3. Check the end date. Migri updates the list every week. An employer is removed when its certification expires and it has not applied for an extension.
  4. Never pay for a job. A real employer does not charge you for a job offer or a permit.

Common questions

Which employers in Finland are certified to hire foreign workers?

The Finnish Immigration Service publishes them. There were 69 certified employers on its list on 20 November 2026, and this page lists them all.

What does a certified employer mean in Finland?

The company has shown that it meets certain requirements as an employer. This speeds up the residence permit application of a person it hires.

Do I still need to apply for a residence permit myself?

Yes. You apply yourself and must meet the requirements for the permit. Certification only speeds up the processing.

Does being on the list mean the company is hiring?

No. The Finnish Immigration Service says the list is not a job advertisement.

How often is the list updated?

Every week.
