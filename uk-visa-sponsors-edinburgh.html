Visa sponsorship companies in Edinburgh

Employers in Edinburgh licensed to sponsor a UK work visa.

1,180 organisations in Edinburgh hold a Home Office licence to sponsor work visas, and 1,111 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route. This page lists them from the official register dated 12 November 2026.

1,180 of the 1,180 have an A rating, which is the full licence. An organisation can be licensed for more than one route, so the rows add up to more than the total.

A licence is not a job offer. Being on the register means the employer is allowed to sponsor. It does not mean it is hiring now, or that a particular vacancy comes with sponsorship. Always check the job ad or ask the employer.

Common questions

How many companies in Edinburgh can sponsor a UK work visa?

1,180 organisations with Edinburgh as their town or city are on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors dated 12 November 2026. 1,111 of them are licensed for the Skilled Worker route.

Does a company on this list have jobs in Edinburgh right now?

Not necessarily. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor workers. It does not show vacancies, and a licensed employer can advertise jobs that do not come with sponsorship.

Which kinds of employers sponsor visas in Edinburgh?

Going by registered names, the largest group among A-rated Skilled Worker sponsors in Edinburgh is hotels, restaurants and food, with 77 organisations. The list also covers many small and medium businesses in other sectors.

Why can I not find a company I know?

The register uses legal names, not brand names, and the town is the one the organisation registered with, often its head office. Search the full register by name to check.
