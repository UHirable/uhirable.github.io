Germany Opportunity Card 2026 (Chancenkarte)

Move to Germany for up to a year to look for a job: who qualifies, the points table and how to apply.

Last checked: November 2026. Visa rules and salary thresholds change every year. This page is a general guide, not legal or immigration advice. Confirm every detail on the German government's official portal before you apply: Make it in Germany

The Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte) lets you move to Germany for up to one year to look for a job, without a job offer. There are two ways to get it: a qualification that is fully recognised in Germany, or at least six points in the points system. In both cases you need to show you can pay for your stay: at least €1,091 net per month in a blocked account in 2026, or a declaration of commitment.

Two ways to qualify

OptionWhat you need
1. Recognised qualificationA foreign vocational or academic qualification that is fully recognised in Germany, or a qualification obtained in Germany. No points and no language proof are required.
2. Points systemA completed vocational or academic qualification recognised in the country where you got it (vocational training of at least two years), German at level A1 or English at level B2, and at least six points.

What you can do with it

  • Look for a job or self-employment in Germany for up to one year.
  • Work part-time, up to 20 hours a week in total.
  • Do trial work for up to two weeks per employer.
  • Extend it for up to two years if you find a qualifying job but cannot get another residence title.

How to apply

  1. Use the official self-check on Make it in Germany to estimate your points.
  2. Get proof of your qualification. For a degree, a positive anabin result or a Statement of Comparability.
  3. Arrange proof of funds: a blocked account or a declaration of commitment.
  4. Apply online through the Consular Services Portal of the Federal Foreign Office. The visa fee is €75.

Questions people ask

How many points do I need for the Opportunity Card?

At least six points, if your qualification is not fully recognised in Germany. If it is fully recognised, or you qualified in Germany, you do not need points.

How much money do I need for the Opportunity Card in 2026?

A blocked bank account with at least €1,091 net per month, or a declaration of commitment from someone in Germany.

Can I work with an Opportunity Card?

Yes. You can do one or more part-time jobs totalling 20 hours a week, and trial work of up to two weeks per employer.

How long is the Opportunity Card valid?

Up to one year for the job search. If you find a qualifying job but cannot get another residence title, it can be extended for up to two years.

Where do I apply?

Online through the Consular Services Portal of the Federal Foreign Office. If you already live in Germany with a valid residence title, contact your local foreigners authority.
