What you get in the free checker

  • Match score against the job ad you paste: skills, years of experience and job title.
  • Missing skills, split into must-have and nice-to-have so you know what actually matters.
  • ATS format check: two-column layouts, tables, images, text boxes, unusual fonts and page count.
  • Years of experience in similar roles compared with what the ad asks for.
  • Line-by-line fixes you can apply in five minutes.

Why no sign-up and no upload matters

Your CV has your name, phone, address and work history. Most resume scanners upload it to their servers and keep it. CV Match runs entirely in your browser: the file is read on your device, scored on your device, and nothing is sent anywhere. Close the tab and it's gone.

When Jobscan is still worth paying for

If you apply to dozens of jobs a week and want a tracker, LinkedIn profile optimisation and cover-letter tools in one subscription, Jobscan's paid plan is a reasonable buy. If you want the core thing (does my CV match this ad, and what is missing), the free checker does that without limits.
