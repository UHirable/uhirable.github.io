Pilbara DAMA labour agreement employers in Australia

Current labour agreements, 30 September 2026.

170 current labour agreements in Australia are of the type "Pilbara DAMA". Each one lets the business sponsor skilled workers from overseas. This page lists them all with their end dates, from the official Home Affairs list dated 30 September 2026.

United KingdomCanadaUnited StatesIrelandNetherlandsDenmarkAustraliaFinland

A DAMA is a formal agreement between the Australian Government and a state, territory or regional authority. It gives employers in that area access to more occupations than the standard skilled migration programme. Labour agreements are generally in effect for five years. Search the full list to check any company.

An agreement is not a job offer. A labour agreement lets the business sponsor skilled workers from overseas. It does not mean it is hiring now. This is the list of labour agreements only. It is not a list of every employer that can sponsor a visa in Australia.

Common questions

Which employers in Australia have a Pilbara DAMA labour agreement?

170 agreements of this type are on the Department of Home Affairs list dated 30 September 2026. This page lists them all.

Does a company on this list have a job for me?

Not necessarily. The list shows who holds an agreement. It does not show vacancies, so check each employer's careers page.

Which visas can be granted under a labour agreement?

The Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482), the Employer Nomination Scheme visa (subclass 186) and the Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 494).
