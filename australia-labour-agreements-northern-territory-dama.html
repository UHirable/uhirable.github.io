A DAMA is a formal agreement between the Australian Government and a state, territory or regional authority. It gives employers in that area access to more occupations than the standard skilled migration programme. Labour agreements are generally in effect for five years. Which employers in Australia have a Northern Territory DAMA labour agreement?

852 agreements of this type are on the Department of Home Affairs list dated 30 September 2026. This page lists them all.

Does a company on this list have a job for me?

Not necessarily. The list shows who holds an agreement. It does not show vacancies, so check each employer's careers page.

Which visas can be granted under a labour agreement?

The Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482), the Employer Nomination Scheme visa (subclass 186) and the Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 494).
