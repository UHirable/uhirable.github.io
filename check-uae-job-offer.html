How to check a UAE job offer is real

Four official checks to do before you sign, travel or pay anyone.

Last checked: 15 November 2026. This page is a general guide, not legal advice. Every step uses an official UAE government service, linked below. Services and menus can change, so follow the official site if it looks different.

A genuine UAE job offer is issued on the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE) offer form, with a transaction number and a company number that you can check online. The employer pays recruitment fees, not you.

Read your offer letter

A valid MOHRE offer letter shows:

  • The MOHRE logo, with a transaction number printed under it
  • The company number (establishment number)
  • Job title, type of contract and salary
  • Weekly leave, notice period and start date

Red flag: no transaction number or no company number. Ask the employer for them before you go any further.

Check the offer on MOHRE

  1. Open the MOHRE enquiry portal: inquiry.mohre.gov.ae (or mohre.gov.ae → Services → Enquiry Services).
  2. Choose Enquiry for Job Offer.
  3. Enter the transaction number and the company number from your letter, and the date range.
  4. For permit type, choose All Work Permits.

Genuine: the system shows your offer, and the job title, company and salary match your copy.

Red flag: no record found, or the details are different from the letter you were sent.

You can also check through the MOHRE app or the MOHRE call centre on 600590000.

Check the company exists

  • Mainland companies: the National Economic Register at growth.gov.ae (Services → Inquire about Economic Licences). It needs a UAE Pass login, so if you are abroad, use the embassy step below.
  • Dubai International Financial Centre: the free DIFC public register, no account needed.
  • Abu Dhabi Global Market: the free ADGM public register.

Never pay a recruitment fee

In the UAE, job seekers are not required to pay any recruitment agency. The fees are the employer's responsibility. Anyone asking you for a "visa fee", "processing fee" or "training fee" to secure the job is a warning sign.

If you are outside the UAE

Contact the UAE embassy or consulate in your country before you travel. Embassy staff can help you check whether the company and the offer are genuine.

After you sign

Keep a copy of the signed offer with its transaction number. Your employer then applies for your work permit and entry visa. The contract you sign later can add benefits, but its terms must match the offer.

Common questions

How do I know if a UAE job offer is real?

A real offer is issued on the MOHRE offer form with a transaction number and a company number. Enter both in the MOHRE enquiry service. If no record appears, the offer may not be genuine or was not submitted properly.

Do I have to pay a recruitment agency to get a job in the UAE?

No. In the UAE, job seekers are not required to pay a recruitment agency. The fees are the employer's responsibility.

Where is the transaction number on my offer letter?

It is the code printed under the MOHRE logo on the offer letter. If it or the company number is missing, ask the employer for it.
