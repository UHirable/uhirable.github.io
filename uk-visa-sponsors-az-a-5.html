Common questions

How do I check if a company can sponsor my UK visa?

Find its legal name on this A to Z list or type it into the register search. If it is listed with the Skilled Worker route, it is licensed to sponsor that visa.

Why is a company I know missing?

The register uses legal names, not brand names. Search for part of the name, or look up the company's registered name on Companies House first.

Does being on the list mean the company is hiring?

No. It means the company is allowed to sponsor workers. Check its current vacancies and whether each one offers sponsorship.
