    The two main work permits

    • General Employment Permit. Covers most jobs, unless the job is on the official list of ineligible occupations. It can be granted for a 12-month contract.
    • Critical Skills Employment Permit. For jobs on the Critical Skills Occupations List, such as ICT, engineering, science and medicine, and for some other well-paid jobs. It needs a job offer of at least 2 years.

    Either you or the employer can apply, and the application must be made at least 12 weeks before the job starts. Salary rules and the occupation lists change, so check the current rules on enterprise.gov.ie.

    How to use this list in your job search

    1. Go to their careers page. Apply on the employer's own site, and look for the words "employment permit" or "visa sponsorship" in the ad.
    2. Check the permit type. Make sure your job and salary fit a General or Critical Skills permit before you apply.
    3. Never pay for a job. A real employer does not charge you for a job offer or a permit.

    Common questions

    Which companies in Ireland sponsor work permits?

    8,411 employers received employment permits from January to October 2026. You can search all of them on this page by company name.

    Which employers get the most permits?

    This year the most permits went to Dawn Meats Ireland UC, Google Ireland Limited, Redwood Extended Care Facility Unlimited Company.

    Does being on this list mean the company is hiring?

    No. It shows permits already issued this year. It does not show vacancies, so always check the employer's current jobs.

    Who applies for an Irish employment permit?

    Either the employer or the worker can apply to the Department of Enterprise, Tourism and Employment, at least 12 weeks before the job starts.

    How often is the list updated?

    The Department publishes a new file every month, covering the year so far. We refresh this page from it.
