H-1B visa sponsors in California

Employers with certified H-1B filings for jobs in California, July 2025 to June 2026.

11,189 employers had Labor Condition Applications certified for H-1B jobs in California from July 2025 to June 2026, with 58,755 certified applications in total. This page ranks them, from US Department of Labor data.

Search all US sponsorsUnited KingdomCanada

The numbers count certified applications where the place of work is in California, whatever state the employer is based in. The cities with the most filings are San Francisco, San Jose, Sunnyvale, Santa Clara.

A certified LCA is not a visa and not an open job. It is the first step an employer takes before filing an H-1B petition, and it also covers extensions and transfers for people already in the US. Use the list to find employers that sponsor regularly, then check their current vacancies.

How to use this list

  1. Target employers that sponsor often. A high number of certified applications means the employer files for foreign workers every year.
  2. Match the job category. The table shows the kinds of jobs each employer filed for. Apply where your occupation is on the list.
  3. Compare the pay. The median salary is what the employer wrote on its filings. Use it when you negotiate.

Common questions

Which companies sponsor H-1B visas in California?

By certified applications from July 2025 to June 2026, the largest are Google LLC, Apple Inc. and Meta Platforms, Inc. 11,189 employers filed for jobs in California in total.

What is an LCA and how is it linked to the H-1B visa?

A Labor Condition Application is filed by the employer with the Department of Labor before it can file an H-1B petition. It states the job, the place of work and the pay. A certified LCA does not guarantee a visa.

Does a company on this list have open jobs now?

Not necessarily. The data shows past filings, including extensions and transfers for current employees. Check the employer's careers page for current vacancies.
