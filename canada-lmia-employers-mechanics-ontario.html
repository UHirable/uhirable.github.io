LMIA employers hiring mechanics in Ontario

Employers in Ontario approved to hire foreign mechanics.

183 employers in Ontario were approved to hire foreign mechanics with a positive LMIA from July 2025 to June 2026. Most are in Mississauga, Brampton and Toronto. This page lists them from the official Government of Canada list published on 30 October 2026.

How to use this list

  1. Pick employers that hired for your job. The last column shows the occupations each employer was approved for.
  2. Go to the employer directly. Look for current vacancies on the employer's own site or on a job site, and apply there.
  3. Never pay for an LMIA. The employer applies for it and pays the fee. A recruiter who asks you to pay for one is a warning sign.

Common questions

Which employers in Ontario hire foreign mechanics with an LMIA?

183 employers in Ontario on the official positive LMIA list for July 2025 to June 2026 had mechanics among their main approved occupations. The table on this page shows those with the most approved positions.

Which cities in Ontario have the most LMIA employers for mechanics?

Counting employers, the top cities are Mississauga, Brampton, Toronto.

Does this list show jobs that are open now?

No. It is a record of past approvals in the last four published quarters. Always check the employer's current vacancies.

Who pays for the LMIA?

The employer applies and pays the processing fee. You should not be asked to pay for an LMIA.
