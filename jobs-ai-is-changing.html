Jobs AI Is Already Changing

Twenty roles, checked against more than one source. For each one we show what the official numbers say, what independent studies add, where they disagree, and what BLS says about the way forward.

AOfficial statistics

US Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) projections for 2025–35 and employment counts.

BResearch and surveys

Anthropic, Stanford, Indeed Hiring Lab, the World Economic Forum and survey data. Sample sizes are noted.

CSecondary reports

Articles that repeat official or research data. Used only to add context, and labeled.

For each role we compared the BLS projection with at least one independent source. "Agree" means they point the same way. "Conflict" means they point opposite ways. "Single source" means we found only BLS.

Why we do not rate jobs by AI exposure alone

  • BLS states that its exposure categories do not imply job loss, and that they are not employment forecasts. [33]
  • Across 831 occupations, BLS found no relationship between an occupation's AI exposure and whether its employment is projected to grow or shrink. [34]
  • Anthropic's own study found no systematic rise in unemployment for highly exposed workers since late 2022. It found the job-finding rate for 22–25-year-olds entering exposed occupations fell about 14%, a result the authors call barely statistically significant. [36]
  • Stanford researchers using ADP payroll data found 22–25-year-olds in the most exposed occupations saw a 16% relative employment decline after controlling for firm-level shocks, while experienced workers stayed stable. [37]
  • The Yale Budget Lab found no sign of economy-wide disruption so far, and warns that some firms may blame AI for layoffs with other causes. [43]

1Computer ProgrammersSources agree: shrinking

What the official numbers say A

110,800 jobs in 2025. BLS projects a 7% decline to 2035 (about 8,100 jobs). Median pay $100,390. [5]

What BLS says about AI

BLS says some higher-skilled programming tasks will likely shift to other workers such as software developers. [5]

Independent signals B

  • Anthropic's task-coverage study ranks computer programmers first among the most exposed jobs, at 75% coverage. [36]
  • The earlier BLS edition (2024–34) also projected a decline, of 6%, so the direction has held across two cycles. [6]
Read with care
  • BLS still expects about 4,400 openings a year from turnover despite the decline. [5]
Where BLS points nextBLS projects software developers to grow 10% (see Software Developers below). [4]
2Software Developers, QA Analysts and TestersSources conflict

What the official numbers say A

1,905,400 jobs in 2025. BLS projects 10% growth to 2035 (185,400 jobs). Median pay $135,980 for developers and $104,300 for QA analysts and testers. QA analysts and testers held about 187,600 jobs. BLS projects the group as one, and we found no separate QA projection. [4]

Independent signals B

  • ADP data analyzed at Stanford shows employment of 22–25-year-old software developers was about 20% below its late-2022 peak by July 2025. [38]
  • In Indeed postings for Q1 2026, 4.5% of software development roles were entry-level and 69.3% were senior. [39]
  • Software postings have risen about 15% since February 2025 but remain 27.5% below pre-pandemic levels, and 71% of the recent increase came from senior roles. [40][48]
Read with care
  • The Stanford authors report employment for experienced workers in the same occupations stayed stable. [37]
3Web Developers and Digital DesignersSources conflict

What the official numbers say A

220,100 jobs in 2025. BLS projects 5% growth to 2035 (11,300 jobs). Median pay $99,520. BLS groups web developers and digital designers in its highest AI exposure tier, alongside customer service representatives. [1][31]

What BLS says about AI

BLS says AI use may soften employment growth, and may let workers in other occupations handle basic web development tasks. [1]

Independent signals B

  • A Bluehost-commissioned survey of 201 US small-business owners (Aug 2026) found 53% built their website with an AI tool, saving an average of about $850 and 32 hours versus hiring a developer. [44]
  • Web-design job ads on Freelancer.com fell 59% between early 2024 and late 2026. [45]
Read with care
  • The Bluehost sample is small and Bluehost sells hosting. The Freelancer.com figure comes from a single marketplace, so it shows pressure on low-cost freelance work, not the whole profession. [44][45]
  • BLS says exposure does not by itself mean job loss, and found no link between exposure and projected growth across 831 occupations. [33][34]

10Graphic DesignersSources agree: shrinking

What the official numbers say A

253,100 jobs in 2025. BLS projects a 2% decline to 2035 (about 4,200 jobs), with about 16,000 openings a year from turnover. Median pay $62,960. The earlier edition projected 2% growth, so the outlook turned negative in one cycle. [2][3]

What BLS says about AI

BLS says AI design tools should make graphic designers more productive and may reduce the need for them. [2]

Independent signals B

  • The WEF Future of Jobs 2025 report places graphic designers just outside its top-10 fastest-declining roles, a first for the series. [41]
Read with care
  • The BLS decline is small. BLS expects designers to still be needed for layouts and branding. [2]
11Customer Service RepresentativesSources agree: shrinking

What the official numbers say A

BLS projects employment falling from about 2.67 million to 2.52 million between 2025 and 2035, a 5.3% decline (141,800 jobs). BLS places this occupation in its highest AI exposure tier. [17][32]

What BLS says about AI

BLS expects advances in speech and language processing and AI to let computers handle more customer interactions. [31]

Independent signals B

  • BLS wage and employment statistics show employment already fell by 130,180 (4.8%) in the year to May 2025. [30]
  • Anthropic's task-coverage study lists customer service representatives among the most exposed jobs. [36]
  • ADP data analyzed at Stanford shows early-career customer service employment about 11% below its November 2022 peak by July 2025. [38]
Read with care
  • A 2026 Robert Half report found high hiring demand for customer service roles even as 10-year forecasts point down. [46]
12Data Entry KeyersSources agree: shrinking

What the official numbers say A

BLS projects a 26% decline to 2035. BLS credits a mix of AI, machine learning, mobile data capture and optical character recognition. [31][32]

Independent signals B

  • Anthropic's task-coverage study finds 67% of data entry keyers' work covered by observed AI use, since reading source documents and entering data is heavily automated. [36]
  • The WEF Future of Jobs 2025 report lists data entry clerks among its fastest-declining roles worldwide. [49][42]
Read with care
  • Coverage describes tasks AI is used for, not jobs already lost. The BLS figure is a projection. [36]
13Secretaries and Administrative AssistantsSources agree: shrinking

What the official numbers say A

For secretaries and administrative assistants (except legal, medical and executive), BLS projects a decline from about 1.89 million to 1.78 million jobs, 6.0% (114,100 jobs). Across all office and administrative support occupations, BLS projects a 4.0% decline (752,100 jobs), the largest drop of any major group. Counting all secretary and assistant subcategories together, one summary of BLS data shows a 2% decline from 3,515,600 jobs. [17][35][18]

What BLS says about AI

BLS says using automation tools, including AI, in workflows is likely to reduce demand for office and administrative support occupations. [17]

Independent signals B

  • The WEF Future of Jobs 2025 report lists administrative assistants and executive secretaries among its fastest-declining roles worldwide. [41]
Read with care
  • The size of the decline depends on which BLS subcategory is counted. [17][18]
14Bookkeeping, Accounting and Auditing ClerksSources agree: shrinking

What the official numbers say A

1,532,400 jobs in 2025. BLS projects a 6% decline to 2035 (85,600 jobs). Median pay $50,670. [19]

Independent signals B

  • The WEF Future of Jobs 2025 report expects 25–35% less demand for accounting, bookkeeping and payroll clerks, and names them among the roles with the greatest reduction in employment. [42]
Where BLS points nextAccountants and auditors are projected to grow 5% (see below). CPA certification may improve prospects, according to BLS. [20]
15Accountants and AuditorsSources conflict

What the official numbers say A

1,595,200 jobs in 2025. BLS projects 5% growth to 2035 (79,400 jobs), with about 115,300 openings a year. Median pay $83,680. [20]

What BLS says about AI

BLS says accountants and auditors may use AI and robotic process automation to increase their productivity. [20]

Independent signals B

  • The WEF Future of Jobs 2025 report lists accountants and auditors among its fastest-declining roles worldwide. [41]
Read with care
  • WEF reflects a global employer survey, while BLS projects US employment, so the two measure different things. [41][20]
16Paralegals and Legal AssistantsSources agree: flat to shrinking

What the official numbers say A

404,900 jobs in 2025. BLS projects little or no change to 2035 (about 1,100 fewer jobs). Median pay $62,890. A summary of BLS data reports a 5% decline for legal secretaries and administrative assistants (about 8,300 jobs from 157,600). [21][23]

What BLS says about AI

A legal-industry summary reports BLS saying demand for paralegals is expected to be limited by advances in technology, including AI. [22]

Independent signals B

  • The WEF Future of Jobs 2025 report places legal secretaries just outside its top-10 fastest-declining roles. [41]

What we could not verify

  • BLS publishes an AI exposure tier for every occupation, but the file was not readable here. We only confirmed that customer service representatives and web developers sit in the highest tier. [32]
  • Roles marked "single source" have no independent cross-check yet. The BLS number stands, but nothing confirms or contradicts it.
  • The BLS projects the software developers and QA testers group as one, so manual QA testers have no separate outlook.
  • Sales development representatives have no BLS category of their own and are not covered.
