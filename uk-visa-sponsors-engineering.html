Common questions

How many engineering companies can sponsor a UK work visa?

842 organisations in this group are on the Home Office register of licensed sponsors dated 11 November 2026. 833 are licensed for the Skilled Worker route.

Where are most of them?

The towns and cities with the most are London, Manchester and Birmingham.

Does a place on this list mean there is a job for me?

No. The register shows who is allowed to sponsor workers. It does not show vacancies, and a licensed employer can advertise jobs that do not come with sponsorship.
